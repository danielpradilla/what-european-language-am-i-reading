# Credits

## Chart

The app follows "Which European language am I reading?" by Øystein H. Brekke ([@oysteib](https://x.com/oysteib)) and Max Fras ([@maxfras](https://x.com/maxfras)), metro-map version, [21 February 2021](https://x.com/maxfras/status/1363473000918626307). Brekke made the original flowchart; Fras had it redrawn as the metro map.

Earlier versions: Brekke's [Latin-only flowchart](https://ebookfriendly.com/which-european-language-reading-flowchart/) and [full flowchart of January 2021](https://imgur.com/what-european-language-am-i-reading-flow-chart-Dw0yg6S), [u/mel_afefon's adaptation](https://www.reddit.com/r/languagelearning/comments/lmwsw7/what_european_language_am_i_reading_european/), and Eamonn O'Brien-Strain's [language phenotype tree](https://eamonn.org/identifying-a-written-language).

## Europe map

- Language coordinates and the language families behind the line colours: [Glottolog](https://glottolog.org) 5 (Hammarström, Forkel, Haspelmath & Bank), licensed CC BY 4.0. Station positions on `europe.html` are schematised by hand from these coordinates.
- Coastline scenery behind the lines: [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) 50m land and lakes, public domain, simplified and warped so the seas fall between the schematic stations.
